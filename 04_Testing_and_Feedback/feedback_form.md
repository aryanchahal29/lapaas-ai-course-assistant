## 1. The 5 Evaluation Questions

When conducting testing sessions with human testers, ask them to interact with the prototype and answer these 5 questions:

1. **Ease of Use:** Was the 6-question discovery flow smooth and easy to navigate? (Rate 1 to 5)
2. **Answer Clarity:** Did the assistant give clear, direct answers to your typed questions without marketing fluff? (Rate 1 to 5)
3. **Course Understanding:** Did the assistant help you understand what you will actually build, how long you get access (3 months), and the self-paced format? (Yes / No / Comments)
4. **Friction / Confusion:** Was anything confusing, misleading, or difficult to understand during your session?
5. **Next Step Likelihood:** Based on what you saw, would you click through to the official course page or ask the mentor on WhatsApp, and why?
