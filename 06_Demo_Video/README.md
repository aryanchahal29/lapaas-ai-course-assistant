1. **Above-the-Fold Landing View:** Shows the 112px masthead with official logo, balanced 12-column hero, and the Chalk White tool band prominently visible on initial load.
2. **Smooth CTA Scroll:** Clicking *"Start the 60-second check"* smoothly scrolls to the tool band and focuses the first option.
3. **Interactive 6-Question Assessment:** Progresses through questions 1 to 6 with real-time Answers Ledger updates on the sticky left rail.
4. **Structured 3-Row Result View:** Demonstrates the customized assessment rows (*Where you would start*, *What you can build*, and *What to know before you enroll*) and official checkout button.

6. **Unverified Query Transparency:** Submits the inquiry *"Do you guarantee a job after completing this course?"*, showing the bot's honest refusal to invent employment guarantees.
7. **Clean Restart:** Uses the *"Restart"* control in the footer to show that the questionnaire and Answers Ledger reset cleanly to Question 1.
