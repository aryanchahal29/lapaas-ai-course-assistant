## 🎙️ Scene-by-Scene Spoken Script

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Conversion Challenge (0:00 - 0:35)
**[Visual: Screen captures top of page at 1440px. The cursor rests near the brand logo and hero headline.]**

> **Speaker:**  
> "Hi everyone, my name is Aryan Kumar. Today, I'm excited to present the **Course Discovery and Readiness Assistant** for the Lapaas AI Product Engineering course.
>
> When prospective students land on `cohort.lapaas.com`, the course offers immense value at just Rs 2,999. But many non-technical freshers, working professionals, and business owners hesitate before purchasing. They ask: *'Do I need coding? What will I actually build? Is there a job guarantee? Will 3 months of access be enough?'*
>
> If they don't get quick, honest answers, they drop off. That's why we built this lightweight, hand-crafted assistant. 
> Notice right away on the first screen: the masthead is compact at 112 pixels, and the hero and tool band fit seamlessly together, so the discovery tool is immediately visible above the fold on load."

---

### Scene 2: The 6-Question Interactive Fit Check (0:35 - 1:20)
**[Visual: Click the Chartreuse button 'Start the 60-second check'. The page smooth-scrolls into the Tool Band. Focus moves to Option 1.]**

> **Speaker:**  
> "Clicking 'Start the 60-second check' brings us straight into the tool band.
>
> Notice the layout architecture: on the left, we have a sticky 4-column rail with the **Answers Ledger**. On the right, an 8-column card that grows naturally with content, with zero nested scrollbars.
>
> Let's answer Question 1: I'll select *'Student or Fresher'*.
> Instantly, the ledger on the left updates with row 1, showing our selected answer and an underlined 'Change' link.
>
> In the panel, only the active question is shown: Question 2, *'What is your primary goal?'* Let's pick *'Job or internship proof-of-work'*.
>
> Now Question 3: *'What is your coding background?'* Notice the 6-segment progress bar at the top tracking our progress, and how the active row in the ledger is highlighted with a 2-pixel Obsidian rule. If I ever want to change a previous answer, I can simply click 'Change' on row 1 or row 2, or use the 'Back' button in the footer.
>
> Let's answer Question 3 as *'Absolute beginner'*, Question 4 as *'Only prompt ChatGPT'*, Question 5 as *'5 to 8 focused hours'*, and Question 6 as *'I have no coding background—will I get stuck?'*."

---

### Scene 3: The 3-Row Assessment & Radical Transparency (1:20 - 1:55)
**[Visual: The Result View appears in the panel. The cursor smoothly scrolls through the three structured rows.]**

> **Speaker:**  
> "Upon answering the sixth question, the panel renders our personalized fit assessment in three clear rows:
>
> 1. **Where you would start:** Since we selected beginner, it directs us to start with *AI & Product Foundations* and *Product Thinking & PRDs*, rather than getting stuck writing code right away.
> 2. **What you can build:** It specifies real outcomes: deployed AI MVPs on Vercel with GitHub proof-of-work. Notice it explicitly disclaims placement guarantees, reminding students that their portfolio is their credential.
> 3. **What to know before you enroll:** It reinforces key operational details: that the course is 100% self-paced in the Edrilla app, access is active for 3 months, and the 30-day refund guarantee requires active participation and lesson implementation per official terms.
>
> Below, high-intent students can click 'Enroll on the official page' or ask further questions."

---

### Scene 4: Instant FAQ & Unverified Guardrails (1:55 - 2:35)
**[Visual: Click 'Ask a question' tab. The 56px tab bar underlines 'Ask a question'. 6 quick questions appear.]**

> **Speaker:**  
> "Next, let's explore the **'Ask a question'** tab.
>
> The empty state displays the six most common questions: fees, placement guarantees, coding prerequisites, self-paced format, refund terms, and language.
>
> Let's click *'Refund Policy'*.
> Immediately, the thread appears, citing the official refund policy link and direct WhatsApp mentor access. The quick questions move neatly behind the 'Common questions' toggle.
>
> Now, let's test what happens if a user asks a claim that is *not* promised on the official page. Let's type: *'Do you give a 100% job placement guarantee?'*
>
> Look at the response: the assistant does not hallucinate or make false promises. It clearly responds: *'I cannot confirm that from the official course details. The official page does not state this information.'* It provides direct links to the official page and Sahil's WhatsApp. This radical honesty protects Lapaas's brand reputation."

---

### Scene 5: Mobile Responsiveness & Business Wrap-Up (2:35 - 3:00)
**[Visual: Toggle device toolbar in Chrome DevTools to 360px mobile view. Scroll down showing mobile ledger and full-width controls.]**

> **Speaker:**  
> "Finally, let's examine mobile responsiveness.
> At 360 pixels, the desktop rail transforms into a native `<details>` dropdown titled *'Your answers (6 of 6)'*. Tabs, options, and input rows scale to full width with zero horizontal clipping.
>
> Best of all, this entire solution runs on **zero external dependencies and zero API costs**. It is 100% client-side JavaScript, meaning Lapaas pays Rs 0 in monthly server or OpenAI API fees.
>
> It qualifies leads, sets honest expectations, and drives higher-converting enrolments.
> Thank you so much for your time and consideration!"
