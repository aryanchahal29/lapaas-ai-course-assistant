# Lapaas AI Product Engineering: Course Analysis

*Date checked: 15 October 2026*  
*Sources: [Official Cohort Page](https://cohort.lapaas.com/) and [Official Refund Policy](https://lapaas.com/refund-policy.html)*

---

## 1. Target Audience
According to the official cohort page, the course is built for three primary groups:
- **Students & Freshers:** Looking to build working AI projects, gain practical shipping experience, and build a proof-of-work portfolio rather than only learning theory.
- **Business Owners:** Looking to automate operational bottlenecks, integrate AI into their business systems, and cut down manual workload.
- **Marketers & Freelancers:** Looking to add AI product engineering, automations, and modern AI stack execution to their client services.

### Who It Is For
- People who want to build and publish real tools.
- People who can commit a few focused hours each week.
- People who prefer practical systems over AI hype.

### Who It Is NOT For
- People looking for a "magic prompt" or quick-rich shortcuts.
- People who cannot commit regular time each week.
- People who only want to binge videos passively without building.
- People wanting pure academic theory without shipping code or tools.
- People unwilling to show their work publicly.

---

## 2. Main Problems the Course Addresses
1. **Tutorial Paralysis & AI Hype:** Many learners watch videos about new AI tools every week but never build a complete, working software product.
2. **Missing Product Engineering Discipline:** Beginners often know how to prompt ChatGPT, but do not know how to connect frontend, backend, databases, APIs, and automated workflows into an MVP.
3. **High Price Barriers of Tech Bootcamps:** Traditional full-stack or data science bootcamps in India cost between Rs 30,000 and Rs 1,50,000, making hands-on engineering guidance unaffordable for many college students.

---

## 3. Major Learning Outcomes Described on the Page
- **Working / Deployed AI Products:** Step-by-step guidance to ship functional MVPs rather than toy prototypes.
- **Proof-of-Work Portfolio:** Live demos, case studies, and code repositories that demonstrate tangible builder capability.
- **A Reusable Builder Method:** A 4-phase framework (*Discover -> Build -> Connect -> Ship*) to evaluate ideas, write PRDs, code with AI assistance, and deploy.
- **Commercial Framework:** A roadmap (*Audit -> Pilot -> System*) to pitch and deliver AI workflows or products to clients or businesses. *(Note: The page explicitly notes that commercial results depend entirely on individual execution, market demand, and customer acquisition).*
- **Curriculum Areas Covered (Names only from the page):**
  1. AI & Product Foundations
  2. Product Thinking & PRDs
  3. AI-Assisted Development
  4. Full-Stack MVP Engineering
  5. Multimodal AI
  6. RAG & Knowledge Layers
  7. MCP, APIs & Integrations
  8. Agents & Automation
  9. Production, Launch & Growth
- **Named Tools & Technologies:** Claude, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Supabase, LangChain, n8n, Gemini, Git/GitHub, Vercel, Docker.
- **Case Walkthroughs Mentioned:** CallTrack (CRM + automation), Autocut (agentic video), Thumbnail Maker, Logoforge, Solopages, Landing Systems. *(Note: The page states Sahil unpacks these systems from products he built himself; it does not claim students build these exact commercial apps).*

---

## 4. Offer, Pricing, and Access Terms
- **Price:** Rs 2,999 one-time payment (GST included).
- **Access Duration:** 3 months of access starting from course activation.
- **Platform:** Hosted inside the proprietary Edrilla web/mobile app (includes video lessons, slide decks, lab files, and assignments).
- **Payment Gateway:** Razorpay (eligible credit/debit cards may display EMI options at checkout).
- **Format:** 100% self-paced. There are no scheduled live lectures or mandatory attendance dates. *(The student reviews shown on the page come from an earlier, completed live cohort and do not indicate upcoming live classes).*
- **Community & Mentorship:** Access to the private Lapaas learner community is included, where students can ask questions and reach out to mentor Sahil Khanna.
- **Mentor Background (According to the page):** 14 years building businesses and teams, with 2,400+ alumni.
- **Certificate:** A Lapaas AI Product Engineering completion certificate is awarded after finishing course requirements. *(The page does not list specific criteria or hours required).*
- **Refund Policy:** The page highlights a "30-day money-back guarantee" directing learners to the [Official Lapaas Refund Policy](https://lapaas.com/refund-policy.html). The policy states that refunds require active participation throughout the period, evidence of implementing the lessons, and a valid business/product use-case where measurable improvement was not achieved.

---

## 5. Three Reasons Someone Might Enrol
1. **Very Accessible Price Point:** At Rs 2,999 all-inclusive, it removes financial risk compared to bootcamps costing tens of thousands of rupees.
2. **Focus on Practical Building Over Theory:** Covers modern developer tools (Cursor, Copilot, Supabase, n8n, Vercel) that let non-coders and beginners ship functional MVPs.
3. **Direct Mentor Access & Clear Scope:** Access to an active community and direct Q&A with Sahil Khanna, backed by a clear 4-phase shipping roadmap.

---

## 6. Three Reasons Someone Might Hesitate
1. **Time-Limited Access (3 Months):** College students facing semester exams or busy working professionals may worry 90 days is too brief to complete the projects.
2. **Strict Refund Conditions:** While marketed as a 30-day guarantee, the official refund policy mandates proof of active implementation and effort, meaning casual dropouts cannot get an unconditional one-click refund.
3. **Self-Paced Format Without Live Handholding:** Beginners who struggle with self-discipline or expect scheduled live classroom lectures might hesitate to buy an asynchronous video-based course.
