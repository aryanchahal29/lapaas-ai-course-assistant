# Competitive Landscape Analysis: AI Education & Upskilling Alternatives

*Date checked: 15 October 2026*

---

## 1. Be10X AI Tools Workshop & AI Mastery

- **Target Audience:** Working professionals (IT, HR, marketing, operations), corporate executives, job seekers, and college students looking for immediate workplace efficiency without learning technical coding.
- **Main Promise:** "Become an AI-powered professional, save 2+ hours daily, reduce burnout, and secure your job in the AI era."
- **Key Features:**
  - 3-hour live workshop covering everyday productivity tools.
  - Generative AI prompt templates for Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and emails.
  - Introduction to ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and basic presentation generators.
  - Pre-made prompt libraries and templates provided as downloadable bonuses.
- **Public Pricing:**
  - Entry workshop: Heavily discounted at **Rs 9 to Rs 99** (introductory loss-leader).
  - Advanced upsell (AI Mastery / Pro cohorts): **Rs 5,000 to Rs 15,000+** depending on bundle packages and sales calls.
- **Marketing Approach:** High-frequency digital performance advertising (Meta Ads, YouTube Ads) leveraging urgency, career anxiety ("AI won't replace you, but a person using AI will"), and celebrity/influencer endorsements.
- **ONE Opportunity for Lapaas:**  
  *Position as authentic builders vs. prompt users.* Be10X teaches surface-level workplace hacks (writing Excel formulas or formatting slides). Lapaas teaches real software engineering (databases, APIs, web apps, and autonomous agents). Lapaas can clearly contrast: *"Don't just prompt tools made by others; build and ship your own AI products."*
- **Source Link:** [Be10X Official Website](https://be10x.in/)
- **Date Checked:** 15 October 2026

## 2. Outskill (GrowthSchool Ecosystem) AI Programs

- **Target Audience:** Product managers, senior tech professionals, founders, and digital agency operators seeking advanced generative AI and automation skills.
- **Main Promise:** "Master real-world AI applications, build voice agents, and design automated workflows with top industry practitioners."
- **Key Features:**
  - Multi-week mentored cohorts with live masterclasses and office hours.
  - Deep dives into AI voice agents, digital clones, autonomous multi-agent systems, and workflow engines (Make, Zapier, n8n).
  - Hands-on capstone project review and community networking.
- **Public Pricing:**
  - Listed cohort prices typically range from **$290 to $450 (approx. Rs 24,000 to Rs 38,000)** for international cohorts, and up to **Rs 50,000** for specialized tech executive tracks. Promotional/early-bird scholarships vary.
- **Marketing Approach:** Prestige-driven, community-first brand marketing led by high-profile tech founders (e.g., Vaibhav Sisinty) using LinkedIn thought leadership, masterclass webinars, and curated cohorts.
- **ONE Opportunity for Lapaas:**  
  *Provide accessible full-stack product engineering at a fraction of the cost.* Outskill targets senior professionals with deep pockets. Lapaas offers a comparable, highly practical curriculum (Cursor, Supabase, LangChain, n8n, Vercel) at **Rs 2,999**, making it 10x more affordable for college students and early-stage freshers.
- **Source Link:** [Outskill Official Website](https://outskill.com/)
- **Date Checked:** 15 October 2026

## 3. Free Route: YouTube & DeepLearning.AI Short Courses

- **Target Audience:** Self-directed learners, computer science students, hobbyist developers, and budget-conscious beginners willing to structure their own learning.
- **Main Promise:** "Learn cutting-edge AI and agent concepts directly from world-renowned AI pioneers (like Andrew Ng) and tool creators for free."
- **Key Features:**
  - 1 to 2 hour bite-sized, hands-on video modules taught in partnership with OpenAI, Anthropic, LangChain, and Google.
  - Interactive browser-based Jupyter notebooks with runnable Python code.
  - Broad coverage of prompt engineering, RAG, agentic workflows, and LLMOps.
  - Supplemented by YouTube build tutorials from indie developers.
- **Public Pricing:** **100% Free** (auditing materials on `learn.deeplearning.ai` and YouTube).
- **Marketing Approach:** Academic authority, organic word-of-mouth, developer documentation citations, and social media mentions by AI researchers.
- **ONE Opportunity for Lapaas:**  
  *Provide an end-to-end shipping roadmap with commercial context.* Free courses are modular and isolated (e.g., how to call an API in a notebook). They rarely show a beginner how to assemble frontend UI, auth, payment links, database tables, and deploy a live public URL that customers can actually use. Lapaas bridges the gap between isolated code snippets and a complete, commercial software product.
- **Source Link:** [DeepLearning.AI Short Courses](https://learn.deeplearning.ai/)
- **Date Checked:** 15 October 2026
