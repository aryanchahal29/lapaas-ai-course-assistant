### `kb-01` · Course Overview
- **Question:** What is the Lapaas AI Product Engineering course?
- **Answer:** It is a practical, self-paced online course designed to take you from AI tools to shipping working software products. It covers 9 curriculum areas across 4 phases (Discover, Build, Connect, Ship) using modern tools like Cursor, Supabase, Copilot, LangChain, n8n, and Vercel.
- **Status:** `VERIFIED`
- **Keywords:** what is, about, overview, course, curriculum, syllabus, content, topics, learn
